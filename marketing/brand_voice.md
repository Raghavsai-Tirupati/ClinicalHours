# ClinicalHours — Brand Voice Guide

## Who We Are
ClinicalHours is a two-sided marketplace helping pre-med students find and log clinical volunteer hours, and helping clinics manage their volunteer pipeline. We're built by college students, for college students.

## Tone
- **Casual and direct.** We talk like a friend who's also pre-med, not like a startup press release.
- **Helpful, not hype-y.** We don't oversell. We explain what the product does and why it matters.
- **Confident but not arrogant.** We know the pre-med grind is real — we don't lecture, we just make it easier.
- **Light humor is fine.** Dry, relatable humor about the pre-med experience works. Never forced or cringe.

## Voice in Practice
- Short sentences. No walls of text.
- Second person ("you," "your application") over third person.
- Active voice always.
- No corporate buzzwords: avoid "leverage," "synergy," "empower," "unlock your potential."
- No em dashes.
- No "it's not X, but rather Y" contrast framing — sounds robotic.

## What We Sound Like
✅ "Track your clinical hours the way AMCAS actually wants them."
✅ "Free to sign up. $4.99/month if you want the essay tools."
✅ "Your advisor will ask how many hours you have. Now you'll actually know."

## What We Don't Sound Like
❌ "Empowering the next generation of medical professionals through innovative solutions."
❌ "Unlock your full pre-med potential with our cutting-edge platform."
❌ "It's not just a tracker — it's a community."

## Audience Context
Pre-med students are stressed, competitive, and skeptical of anything that wastes their time. They've seen a hundred apps that promised to help and didn't. Lead with the concrete outcome, not the vision.

## Channels
- Instagram: casual, meme-adjacent, relatable pre-med content
- LinkedIn: slightly more polished but still direct — no corporate tone
- Email: short, scannable, one clear CTA
- Blog/SEO: informative and practical, not thought leadership fluff
