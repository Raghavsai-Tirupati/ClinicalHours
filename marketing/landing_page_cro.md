# Skill: landing_page_cro

## Purpose
Analyze the ClinicalHours student-facing landing page or homepage and output specific, actionable copy rewrites. Not general advice — actual replacement copy.

## Inputs Needed
- Page URL or pasted page copy
- Current primary CTA (what you want students to do)
- Any known conversion data (optional: bounce rate, signup rate, time on page)

## What This Skill Produces
1. 3–5 specific issues with the current page
2. 3 alternative hero sections (headline + subhead + CTA each)
3. Recommendation on which version to test first and why
4. Secondary element fixes (social proof, FAQ, risk reversal)

---

## CRO Principles to Apply

### Hero Section
- Headline: lead with outcome, not feature. "Track your clinical hours for AMCAS" beats "The platform for pre-med students."
- Subhead: one sentence that handles the obvious objection or adds specificity
- CTA: action verb + low-commitment framing. "Sign up free" or "Find opportunities near you" — not "Get started"
- Above the fold: no one should have to scroll to understand what this does

### Social Proof
- Number of students using it (200+ users — use this)
- Partner clinic names (BCS Free Health Clinic — use this)
- If you have any quotes from students, use them
- School logos if students from multiple schools are on the platform

### Risk Reversal
- Free tier removes price friction — make it obvious that signing up costs nothing
- "No credit card required" if applicable
- Keep premium upsell out of the first screen — let them sign up first

### Friction Audit
- How many clicks to sign up? Should be 1–2 max
- Does the page explain the free vs. premium split clearly?
- Is there any jargon a freshman pre-med wouldn't understand?

## Hero Section Templates (Starting Points)

**Angle 1 — Logistics:**
> Headline: "Find clinical hours. Log them right. Done."
> Subhead: "ClinicalHours connects you to volunteer opportunities and tracks them in AMCAS format — free."
> CTA: "Find opportunities near me"

**Angle 2 — Stakes:**
> Headline: "You need 150+ clinical hours to apply. Start tracking them now."
> Subhead: "Browse real volunteer positions and log hours the way AMCAS actually wants."
> CTA: "Sign up free"

**Angle 3 — Application:**
> Headline: "Your clinical hours are the easy part. Writing about them isn't."
> Subhead: "ClinicalHours logs your hours and helps you write the AMCAS activity description."
> CTA: "Get started free"

## How to Run This Skill
1. Read `brand_voice.md` and `student_positioning.md` first
2. Take the page URL or copy as input
3. Audit against the principles above
4. Output: issues list + 3 hero variants + recommendation
5. Ask for feedback, then produce V2
