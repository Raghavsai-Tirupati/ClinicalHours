# Skill: social_writer

## Purpose
Write Instagram and LinkedIn posts for the ClinicalHours account that attract pre-med students, build brand awareness, and drive signups — without sounding like a startup.

## Inputs Needed
- Platform: Instagram or LinkedIn
- Post goal: awareness, engagement, or conversion
- Topic or hook idea (optional — skill can generate from scratch)
- Any recent news, milestones, or product updates to incorporate (optional)

## What This Skill Produces
- 2–3 post variations for the chosen platform
- Caption copy (with line breaks formatted for mobile)
- Hashtag set (Instagram only)
- Recommended post format (text-only, carousel, graphic, reel hook)

---

## Platform Guidelines

### Instagram
**Audience:** Pre-med undergrads, 18–22, casual scrolling
**Tone:** Casual, relatable, dry humor welcome, short
**Formats that work:**
- Meme-style graphics with one punchy line
- Carousels: "5 things no one tells you about clinical hours" style
- Text-over-image with a stat or fact
- Reels hook (first line of text shown before "more")

**Caption structure:**
- Line 1: Hook (this is what shows before "more" — make it stop the scroll)
- Line 2–4: The actual content, short and punchy
- Line 5: CTA (soft — "link in bio" or "sound familiar?")
- Hashtags: 5–8, mix of niche (#premed #medschool #clinicalhours) and broad (#premedicine #futuredoctor)

**What works for this audience:**
- Specific numbers ("the average applicant has 150+ clinical hours")
- Relatable stress content ("when your advisor asks how many hours you have and you panic")
- Practical tips they can use immediately
- Mild self-aware humor about the pre-med experience

### LinkedIn
**Audience:** Pre-med students who are on LinkedIn for internships/networking, slightly more professional mode
**Tone:** Still direct and casual, but no memes — more "here's something useful"
**Formats that work:**
- Short text posts (no image needed): insight or tip + soft CTA
- Milestone announcements (competition wins, new partners, user count)
- Founder perspective posts (brief, personal, specific)

**Caption structure:**
- Line 1: Bold statement or question (hook)
- Lines 2–5: Expand the point in 2–3 short paragraphs
- Last line: CTA or question to drive comments
- No hashtag spam — 2–3 max

## Content Buckets (rotate through these)

| Bucket | Description | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Education | AMCAS tips, clinical hour facts, competency explainers | Both |
| Relatability | Pre-med stress, common mistakes, honest takes | Instagram |
| Social proof | User milestones, clinic partners, competition wins | Both |
| Product | Feature highlights, how-to, what's new | Both |
| Community | Pre-med questions, polls, "what's your experience" | Instagram |

## Sample Posts

### Instagram — Education (Carousel hook)
> Slide 1: "5 things pre-meds get wrong about clinical hours"
> Slide 2: "1. Shadowing doesn't count as clinical."
> Slide 3: "2. You need to document the role, not just the hours."
> Slide 4: "3. AMCAS asks for total hours — not weekly."
> Slide 5: "4. The activity description matters more than the number."
> Slide 6: "5. Most students figure this out in junior year. Don't be most students."
> Caption: "Save this before you make one of these. ClinicalHours tracks all of it — free. Link in bio."

>
