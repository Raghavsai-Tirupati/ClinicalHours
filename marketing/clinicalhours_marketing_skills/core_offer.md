# ClinicalHours — Core Offer Sheet

## What It Is
A two-sided marketplace connecting pre-med students with clinical volunteering opportunities, and giving clinics tools to manage their volunteer pipeline.

## Student Side (B2C)

### Free Tier
- Browse and apply to clinical volunteer positions
- Basic hour logging
- Four-step application flow

### Premium Tier — $4.99/month
- AMCAS activity description writer (AI-assisted)
- AAMC core competency tracker
- Essay tools for medical school applications
- Priority features as platform grows

### Who It's For
- Pre-med undergraduates (freshman through senior)
- Post-bacc and gap year students
- Anyone building hours for medical school applications
- Students who use AMCAS and need their clinical hours documented properly

### Core Student Pain Points
1. Finding legitimate clinical opportunities is time-consuming and scattered
2. Logging hours in a format AMCAS actually accepts is confusing
3. Most students have no idea how many hours they have until it's too late
4. Writing the AMCAS activity description for clinical experience is a pain

### Student Value Prop (one sentence)
Find clinical hours, log them the way AMCAS wants, and get the essay help — all in one place.

## Clinic Side (B2B)

### Clinic Portal — $750–$1,000/year
- Applicant sorting and filtering
- Mass email to applicants
- Interview scheduling
- Position management dashboard
- Volunteer history and reporting

### Who It's For
- Free health clinics
- Community health centers (FQHCs)
- Urgent care and specialty clinics that take volunteers
- Any clinic overwhelmed by manual volunteer coordination

### Clinic Value Prop (one sentence)
Stop managing volunteer applications over email — sort, schedule, and communicate from one dashboard.

## Current Traction
- 200+ organic student users
- BCS Free Health Clinic: first partner
- Agape Clinic (DFW): in pipeline
- Live platform on Next.js + Supabase
- Gmail OAuth integration for clinic admins
