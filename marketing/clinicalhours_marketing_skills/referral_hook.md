# Skill: referral_hook

## Purpose
Generate word-of-mouth and referral messaging that gets pre-med students to share ClinicalHours with classmates, GroupMe chats, Discord servers, pre-med clubs, and Reddit threads.

## Inputs Needed
- Channel: GroupMe/text, Discord, Reddit, pre-med club email, in-person
- Any current incentive for referrals (if none, this skill will suggest one)
- Any specific trigger event (exam season, AMCAS opens, application season)

## What This Skill Produces
- 2–3 referral message templates per channel
- A suggested referral incentive structure (if none exists)
- Reddit/Discord organic share strategy (non-spammy)
- Pre-med club pitch blurb (for club presidents to share)

---

## Referral Context

### Why Pre-Meds Share Things
- They share things that make them look helpful to their friend group
- They share things that solve a problem they just discovered
- They share things that give them an edge (or help friends who aren't competitors)
- They do NOT share things that feel like ads

### Best Trigger Moments
- When AMCAS opens (late May/early June) — everyone scrambles to count hours
- When pre-med advisors bring up clinical hours requirements
- When a friend asks "where do I find clinical volunteer opportunities"
- After an MCAT or midterm when students think about their application timeline

## Message Templates

### GroupMe / iMessage (student to student)
> "hey if you're pre-med and haven't tracked your clinical hours yet, there's this free app called ClinicalHours that logs them in AMCAS format. i've been using it — clinicalhours.org"

> "random but if you need clinical volunteer hours there's a platform called ClinicalHours that actually has open positions near campus. free to sign up"

> "my friend built this app for pre-meds to find and log clinical hours — way easier than spreadsheets. clinicalhours.org if you want to check it out"

### Discord (pre-med server, organic drop)
> "not sure if anyone's used this but there's a platform called ClinicalHours that lists actual clinical volunteer opportunities and tracks your hours in AMCAS format. it's free. worth checking out if you're still building hours — clinicalhours.org"

*(Note: only drop this when the question comes up naturally — "where do I find clinical hours" or "how do I track hours for AMCAS")*

### Reddit (r/premed — organic, value-first)
**Strategy:** Answer a real question about clinical hours, mention ClinicalHours as a tool at the end.
> Comment template: "I'd look for free health clinics in your area — they usually take pre-med volunteers and the experience is solid for AMCAS. If you want to track what you find, there's a platform called ClinicalHours that logs hours in AMCAS format. It's free and has volunteer positions listed if your area has any."

*(Don't post promotional threads — only reply to existing questions)*

### Pre-Med Club Email (for club president to forward)
> Subject: Free tool for tracking clinical hours
>
> Hey everyone,
>
> Sharing this because a few people asked me about tracking clinical hours for AMCAS.
>
> There's a platform called ClinicalHours (clinicalhours.org) that lets you find volunteer positions near campus and log your hours in AMCAS format. It's free. They also have premium tools for writing the AMCAS activity description if you want that.
>
> Worth bookmarking before application season.
>
> [Name]
