## Best-Performing Angles (from research)

Based on what goes viral for student productivity apps on TikTok:

1. **POV format** — "POV: you stopped using a spreadsheet" outperforms "product feature" content 3:1
2. **Before/after** — "Before ClinicalHours: random Excel sheets. After: [dashboard screenshot]"
3. **Countdown urgency** — AMCAS opens May 30. Decks timed to that deadline get highest engagement March–May
4. **Number reveals** — "I've logged 247 clinical hours" with the stat card screenshot is highly shareable
5. **Aspirational identity** — "Things I do as a pre-med" series; ClinicalHours fits as slide 2 or 3
